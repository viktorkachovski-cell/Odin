The highest-impact changes

  1. Give the task title room. Keep completion separate from opening a task; show the list and deadline on distinct lines. Show names in shared views and keep Today compact.
  2. Make filters visible. The current rail hides No deadline, Date range and Blocked only to the right. A sheet exposes every choice, with an active-filter count and a clear reset.
  3. Clarify whose work is counted. Household totals and personal work use different scopes. Keep both, with a clearer hierarchy.
  4. Reduce task-entry clutter. Title and list first, optional assignee/date/time/notes in one expandable section.

Tradeoff: clearer rows use more space. Filters and collapsed optional fields require an extra tap. The prototype makes those choices reviewable.

Checked, with limits

The mobile template’s device chrome stays intact; its dark icons are not Android app evidence. Full native actions are not all simulated.
